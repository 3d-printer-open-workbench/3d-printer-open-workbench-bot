# Brand guide (example)

- **Shop name:** set `WORKBENCH_SHOP_NAME` in `.env`.
- **Voice:** warm, clear, honest. No invented reviews, discounts or claims.
- **Colors:** one primary product color, dark grey text, off-white background, one accent.
- **Typography:** a clean sans-serif for headings, a monospace for technical codes.
- **Never:** let AI draw measurements, prices or materials into images; they are printed from real data.
