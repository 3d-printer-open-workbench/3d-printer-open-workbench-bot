Create a clean 3:4 vertical poster for the product in IMAGE 1.

Layout:
- Upper half (~50%): a realistic, calm product photograph of the product from IMAGE 1, kept exactly as it is (same
  shape, details, proportions and color — do not redesign it). Soft natural light on a neutral surface.
- A thin horizontal rule separates the halves. Leave the narrow strip just under the photo EMPTY (no text) — a header
  line will be added later from real data.
- Lower half (~50%) on off-white paper: a restrained technical line drawing inspired by the product's silhouette, a
  few thin guide lines and a small dotted grid patch.

Palette: one color taken from the product, dark grey lines, off-white paper. No gradients, no neon.

Typography: no words, no numbers, no logo, no brand name. Measurements and material are NEVER drawn by the AI; they
are printed afterwards from verified product data.
